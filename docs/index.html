The core idea (pool table vs. chalkboard)

A vanilla transformer is a chalkboard. Each layer writes new numbers in chalk; to make room, old information gets erased. By layer 6, what happened in the first 100 tokens is mostly gone unless attention specifically grabbed it.

Φ-Plasma-Core is a pool table. Each token isn't a number — it's a billiard ball with position AND momentum. The "layers" aren't acts of writing — they're physical time-steps in a billiards game. Balls roll, hit each other, exchange energy, but the system obeys conservation laws: total energy is preserved, and the volume occupied by all balls in their position+momentum coordinates never changes. This is Liouville's theorem from classical mechanics, and we encoded it directly into the architecture.

Why this matters: the architecture is reversible by construction. Information from token 1 is physically still in the phase space at layer 6 — it has to be, because the dynamics conserve phase-space volume. Vanilla transformers have no such conservation; they can and do lose information.

Three mathematical ingredients, each load-bearing

1. Phase-space split (Hamilton's mechanics)

Each token x ∈ ℝᵈ splits into (q, p) with q, p ∈ ℝ^{d/2}. q is "position" (content), p is "momentum" (rate-of-change). Same total dimensionality, organized so conservation laws make sense.

2. Symplectic integration (Marsden-West, 1968)

Each layer is one Störmer-Verlet leapfrog step under a learned Hamiltonian H(q, p, a) = ½p^T M^{-1} p + V(q, a): 

p_{1/2} = p − (h/2) · ∂V/∂q          # half-step momentum
q'      = q + h · M⁻¹ · p_{1/2}     # full-step position
p'      = p_{1/2} − (h/2) · ∂V/∂q'   # half-step momentum at new q

Störmer-Verlet is a 200-year-old integrator from orbital mechanics (used to simulate the solar system for millions of years). It preserves a modified energy function exactly forever, by construction.

3. Hecke-algebra head structure

The 11 attention heads (= Lucas number L(5)) index the basis of the Iwahori-Hecke algebra of type A_{n-1} at parameter q = φ (the golden ratio). A small policy network outputs per-token a soft "Hecke word" mixing head outputs through the algebraic generators. Heads can only mix according to a rule book the algebra provides.

v0.2 — Φ-CONCENTRATE

After a deep audit of 30 prior phi-family models retired by the math audit, we extracted the 5 keepers and composed them into a single architecture. Two of those keepers are rigorous mechanistic-interpretability probes buried inside an abandoned model that nobody knew were rigorous:

  • Koopman EDMD probe — polynomial-lift EDMD with exact spectral diagnostics (gap + Lyapunov via torch.linalg.eig).
  • Williams-Beer PID synergy probe — Gaussian MMI synergy estimator. Can be enforced as a training objective without breaking NLL.

Both are documented in paper/probes/main.tex ("Buried Treasure"). v0.2 inherits 1.93× of plasma's 2.31× stability while gaining the three probes — Pareto-positive.

Honest limitations

  • Scale. 15-42M params. Production LLMs are 1B-1T.
  • Datasets. Only WikiText-2.
  • Baseline. Stripped vanilla — no RoPE, no FlashAttention, no modern tokenizer.
  • Compute. Plasma is ~1.8× slower per step than vanilla due to the symplectic double-gradient.
  • Generation. Only perplexity measured. Downstream task performance untested.

This is a research preview, not a production model.
